Reference Books

On Computer Vision and Machine Learning

Chris Bishop, Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning (amazon) - A benchmark

Simon Prince, Computer Vision (amazon) - Intuitive

Kevin Murphy, Machine Learning: A Probabilistic Perspective (amazon) - Comprehensive

David Marr, Vision (amazon) - The Classic (there is more to deep learning)

Berthold Horn, Robot Vision (amazon) - A Classic (when you really want to know)

Gene Golub, Matrix Computations (amazon) - A Classic on vectors, matrices, tensors

On Deep Learning

Ian Goodfellow, Deep Learning (amazon)

Richard Sutton, Reinforcement Learning (amazon)

On Visual Perception and Cognition - A World Model is more than Large Language Models

Richard Gregory, Eye and Brian (amazon) - For the curious, AI is more than Large Language Models, a world model for reasoning and understanding is more than language models. Gregory's thesis explains the role of mind's eye in visual understanding and interpretation. An insight to the contemporary take on how to satisfy and balance between long-term memory (a `foundation' model of past experience) and short-term decision making (test-time reasoning in interacting with a physical world), e.g. Agentic RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation), Agentic Reinforcement Learning, in the spirit of Daniel Kahneman's Thinking Fast and Slow (amazon).

Richard Gregory, The Intelligent Eye (amazon) - If you want more, this is the 1967-68 Royal Institution Christmas lectures by Gregory
